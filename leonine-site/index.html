About

I’m Leo, and LEONINE is just me. I was born in late 2007 and I’ve always had this drive to create, it doesn’t really matter what.

I shoot on 35mm film and nothing else, on a Nikon F4s. No screen on the back to check, no second go. You press it and wait to find out what you got.

Most of it gets taken when I’m travelling, all over the place, and closer to home around the UK. The grain, the dust, the scratches, the blur, it all stays in. It’s the real, raw side of it. Nothing flashy, just humble and a bit moody.

I won’t tell you what any of it means. That’s up to you.

Imperfection is perfection.
