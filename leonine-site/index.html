About

Hi, I’m Leo, a photographer born in late ‘07 and raised in the south of England, in Hampshire.

My whole life I’ve had a drive to be creative, even if that meant modelling clay into all sorts of contraptions and weird things, or building the next generation of fighter jets. In Lego, of course.

My love for art has really developed over the last few years. Now I’m a young adult with a passion for sharing my best work, so I put this together to show off some of the very best.

Everything is shot on film. It’s like a bad addiction: once you get into film nothing can bring you back, but I like it. If anything it’s a better addiction to have than itching for nicotine at the ripe age of 19 (not pointing fingers, I love you all). Film has become my main medium because it makes me slow down and think about each shot, rather than spamming out 50 images of the same thing where 90% are out of focus.

I also have a drive to create music, though nothing good enough has come of it to post here yet, so that’s to come.

Really, this site is just a centre, a home for everything I’m proud of that I’ve churned out of my… interesting ideas.

Feel free to contact me on any platform. At the moment I’ll cover events for free while I try to get my name out there. Feedback on my work is welcome too. As long as you’re not just bullying me and you’re telling me something handy, I’ll take it.

Imperfection is perfection.

Thanks for having a little read.

Leo, creator and owner of LEONINE Photography
